Evolution of Agentic Engineering

A working model by Orlando Garcia
September 2026

A model for reasoning about how software engineering may evolve from viable agentic engineering actors toward cooperating systems — and how engineering environments determine which variants can persist and scale.

The model

From agentic actors to higher-order engineering systems

01

APPARITION

The object comes into existence: model, context, tools, execution, permissions and feedback combine into a viable agentic engineering actor.

02

SELECTION

WORKING HYPOTHESIS

Engineering environments filter viable variants. Configurations that better fit their environment are more likely to remain useful as scale and autonomy increase.

03

COOPERATION

Selected objects cooperate when cooperation improves their ability to succeed or persist relative to isolated operation.

04

SPECIALIZATION

Persistent differentiated cooperation can make the cooperating system itself the meaningful engineering object — a higher-order object.

VARIATION / MUTATION · MECHANISMOnce the object appears, configurations vary. Those versions supply the population upon which Selection can operate; variation is not a separate stage.
Working hypothesis: agentic engineering may be entering Selection.
Selection

The environment becomes part of the fitness function

The useful question is not which agent variant will win, but which engineering conditions allow useful configurations to operate reliably and economically at scale.

Candidate DevEx conditions for investigation, not an organizational roadmap.

CONTEXTCan agents efficiently acquire sufficient knowledge?
EXECUTIONCan agents safely and reproducibly perform engineering work?
VERIFICATIONCan agents produce trustworthy evidence of correctness?
COORDINATIONCan parallel work compose without coordination cost dominating?
OBSERVABILITYCan humans understand behavior, outcomes and exceptions?
ECONOMICSDo compute, tools, CI and human attention remain viable?
LEARNINGDo failures improve the environment for future executions?
Selection = variation × environmental pressure × differential fitness
The forcing function

What breaks at 1K agents?

1K agents is not a utilization target or industry benchmark. It is a stress test: small inefficiencies in context, execution, verification, coordination, observability, economics and learning become systemic when multiplied across an agent population.

1K-agent scale = amplify( friction + ambiguity + risk + coordination + cost )
Potential recursion

Specialization may create the next object

If persistent cooperation produces a higher-order engineering object, that object can itself vary and encounter environmental selection pressure. The model may therefore be recursive rather than a one-way maturity ladder.

Apparition→Selection→Cooperation→Specialization→Higher-order object→…

Falsifiability

What would challenge the model?

  • Agentic engineering scales reliably without the proposed environmental conditions becoming material constraints.
  • Variation among viable configurations does not encounter meaningful environmental selection pressure.
  • Cooperation consistently adds coordination cost without improving engineering fitness.
  • Persistent differentiated cooperation does not produce stable higher-order engineering objects.
Origins

A corrected application of the evolutionary lens

v0.2 interprets Daniel San Martín's structure as Apparition → Selection → Cooperation → Specialization. The key correction from v0.1 is that mutation/variation supplies versions of the object for Selection; it is a mechanism, not an independent stage. Specialization is also sharpened: persistent cooperation may constitute the object of the next level.

v0.1 remains part of the research history. v0.2 is the current working model, not a prediction or established evolutionary law.
