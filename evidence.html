What we have not observed

Absence matters too

The current evidence is strongest for Selection and increasingly informative at its boundaries with Cooperation and operational Specialization.

Open evidence gaps
  • No accepted source establishes operation of 1,000 concurrent coding agents. 1K remains a forcing function, not an industry benchmark.
  • Uber provides an operational Specialization signal, but does not establish that repeated Cooperation caused specialization.
  • Comparable compute-cost evidence remains sparse outside a small number of sources.
  • Learning loops remain less documented than execution and coordination infrastructure.
  • Contradictory evidence should be added here with the same prominence as supporting evidence when it appears.
