Research synthesis

Traceable interpretations across the incorporated evidence corpus. Findings are working research conclusions, not new facts: each remains connected to its supporting records and exposes what the corpus still cannot establish.

F01 · C02

Engineering environment increasingly explains agentic fitness

Across the incorporated corpus, scaling agentic engineering is repeatedly associated with conditions outside model capability alone: context, execution, verification, coordination, observability, economics, and learning shape whether agentic systems can operate reliably and economically.

What remains uncertain

The corpus does not yet isolate the causal contribution of individual environmental conditions or establish which conditions dominate across different agent architectures.

F02 · C03

Verification feedback can change agent behavior

Cooperation is no longer represented only by multi-agent or system architecture. Incorporated evidence includes a feedback loop in which independent verification components alter a coding agent's subsequent behavior and are associated with reported improvements in working outcomes.

What remains uncertain

Evidence remains insufficient to quantify cooperation advantage relative to useful isolated operation after accounting for coordination and execution cost.

F03 · C03 · C04

The meaningful engineering object may be expanding beyond the coding agent

Several sources describe useful outcomes produced by coding agents embedded in broader systems of context services, orchestration, verification, and platform infrastructure. This is consistent with a shift toward system-level engineering capabilities rather than isolated coding-agent performance as the unit of analysis.

What remains uncertain

The corpus does not yet establish persistent differentiated cooperation that forms a higher-order engineering object; C04 therefore remains an open research frontier.
