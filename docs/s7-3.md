# S7.3 — Claim evaluation

S7.3 derives a reproducible evaluation of each working-model claim from the explicit evidence relationships introduced in S7.2.

## Question

Given the evidence currently incorporated and explicitly mapped to C01–C04, what can the research system say about the evidential state of each claim without pretending that evidence records are votes?

## Status vocabulary

- `UNOBSERVED` — no incorporated evidence is explicitly mapped to the claim.
- `EMERGING` — at least one record SUPPORTS or REFINES the claim, with no mapped contradiction or inconclusive relationship.
- `CONTESTED` — the corpus is unresolved: positive evidence coexists with INCONCLUSIVE evidence, or the available mappings are only inconclusive.
- `CHALLENGED` — at least one incorporated record explicitly CONTRADICTS the claim.

A contradiction dominates the status. Ten supporting records do not erase one contradictory observation.

## What is not a score

The evaluator reports relationship counts, producer diversity, provenance counts, and the underlying evidence records. These are descriptors of the corpus, not numeric weights. The evaluator deliberately does not calculate a confidence percentage, average verdict, majority vote, or truth score.

The current policy does not claim that five first-party engineering blogs outweigh one controlled paper, or vice versa. Provenance, independence, scope, methodology, and limitations remain inspectable evidence properties. A later research decision can refine the evaluation policy without changing the underlying evidence or mappings.

## Reproducibility

`model/evaluation-policy.yaml` declares the evaluation semantics. `scripts/evaluate-model-claims.py` deterministically derives the evaluation from:

1. `model/claims.yaml`
2. `model/evidence-claims.yaml`
3. `evidence/*.yaml`

No LLM call or manual scoring occurs during evaluation.

## Current boundary

S7.3 creates the evaluation engine and verifies its semantics. It does not add a public model-evaluation UI and does not persist a second canonical evaluation dataset. The evaluation is derived state and can always be regenerated from the canonical research corpus.
