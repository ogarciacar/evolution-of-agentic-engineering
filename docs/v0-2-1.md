# v0.2.1 — Model contract

v0.2.1 makes the accepted v0.2 conceptual correction the canonical model claim contract.

## Canonical stages

The model now has four stages:

1. Apparition
2. Selection
3. Cooperation
4. Specialization

Mutation / variation is a mechanism operating after Apparition, not a stage.

## Claim identity

Existing semantic claim IDs are preserved where the underlying stage remains:

- C01 — Apparition
- C03 — Selection
- C04 — Cooperation
- C05 — Specialization

C02 is retired from the active contract because it encoded Mutation as an independent stage. IDs are intentionally not renumbered: historical identity is more important than contiguous numbering.

The exact v0.1 claim contract is preserved under `model/history/v0.1-claims.yaml`.

## Scope boundary

This slice changes only the active model claim contract and its schema. It does not remap evidence or modify the evidence stage vocabulary yet.

As a result, evidence records and `model/evidence-claims.yaml` remain v0.1-era research material until v0.2.2 explicitly reviews and remaps them. Existing mappings to C01, C03, C04, and C05 retain their identifiers but must not be assumed to establish the sharpened v0.2 claims without review.

The evaluator and public website remain migration consumers and are addressed in later slices.
