# S7.4 — Evaluate the model

S7.4 publishes the claim-level evaluation created in S7.3.

## Public surface

`evaluate.html` exposes C01–C05 in model order with their current deterministic evidential state and the mapped evidence behind each state.

The page is generated by `scripts/build-model-evaluation.py` from the same canonical inputs as the evaluator. The public evaluation is therefore derived state, not a manually maintained interpretation layer.

## Research behavior

The surface deliberately makes gaps and unresolved evidence visible. `UNOBSERVED` is presented as an evidence gap rather than evidence against a claim. `CONTESTED` remains visibly unresolved. If a future mapping is `CONTRADICTS`, the corresponding claim becomes `CHALLENGED` even when other records support it.

Counts are shown to make the derivation inspectable, not to imply voting or confidence percentages. Every mapped record links to its durable Scale Signal page.

## Build invariant

Evidence integrity regenerates `evaluate.html` and fails if the committed page differs from the canonical claims, mappings, evidence, or evaluation policy. Changing the research corpus can therefore change the public evaluation without introducing a second canonical dataset.
