# S7.2 evaluation boundary

S7.2 records evidence-to-claim relationships. It does not collapse those relationships into a current claim status.

A claim with three `SUPPORTS` relationships and one `CONTRADICTS` relationship is not automatically "supported". Evidence provenance, independence, scope, recency, limitations, and contradiction cannot be reduced to vote counting without an explicit evaluation rule.

S7.3 may derive a deterministic claim-level research view, but that rule must be defined separately and must preserve contradictory and inconclusive evidence rather than hiding it behind a score.
