# v0.2 evidence → claim mapping decisions

The v0.2 ledger reassesses every incorporated record against the corrected claim semantics. Relationships are not inherited from v0.1 and are not inferred from stage tags.

- **C01 / Apparition** retains direct support from the Anthropic session study because it documents agents performing meaningful multi-action engineering work at large observational scale.
- **C03 / Selection** maps evidence only where engineering conditions, comparisons, runtime constraints, evaluation criteria, economics, or other environmental properties bear on the relative viability of agentic configurations. Variation is a precondition for Selection, not a separate stage.
- **C04 / Cooperation** now asks a stronger question than whether orchestration or parallel agents exist: does cooperation improve the ability of the object to succeed or persist compared with useful isolated operation? Cursor Agents Window, Spotify, and Uber therefore become `INCONCLUSIVE` for C04 where cooperation exists but the record does not isolate a durable cooperation advantage. Symphony remains `SUPPORTS` because the source reports an outcome improvement after moving coordination into an orchestrated system. pstack `REFINES` the claim by showing that cooperation has workload-dependent coordination cost and can be less fit than isolated execution.
- **C05 / Specialization** remains `INCONCLUSIVE` for Uber and pstack. Differentiated roles or task-specific agents do not by themselves establish persistent integrated cooperation that forms a higher-order engineering object.

Two evidence records previously tagged with the v0.1 Mutation stage are remapped to Selection. Their observations remain unchanged; only the model-relative interpretation changes. The Teresa Torres record is also reworded where its interpretation previously treated Mutation as a stage.

The v0.1 mapping decisions remain recoverable from repository history. This document describes the active v0.2 interpretation.
