Test new evidence against the model

A protocol for humans and AI agents to place a new article, paper, engineering report or product announcement against the Evolution of Agentic Engineering without turning interpretation into evidence.

Scope gate

First ask whether it belongs here.

Does the source contain observations about software engineering involving AI or coding agents, agentic engineering systems, or the engineering environments that support them?

YES → Continue with Acquire → Observe → Map → Interpret → Test → Bound.

NO → OUT OF SCOPE. Stop. Do not force a stage or Selection-condition mapping. A useful analogy from another domain is not evidence for or against this model.
The protocol

Observation first. Interpretation second.

01 · ACQUIRE

Read the source and this model. Prefer the original first-party source when available.

02 · OBSERVE

Extract only what the source actually reports. Preserve concrete numbers, constraints and uncertainty.

03 · MAP

Identify relevant evolutionary stages and Selection conditions. Classification is interpretation, not a claim by the source.

04 · INTERPRET

Explain why the observations may relate to those stages or conditions. Do not infer scale or causality that is not reported.

05 · TEST

Classify the evidence as supports, refines, contradicts or inconclusive relative to the working model.

06 · BOUND

State explicitly what the evidence does not establish and what would need to be observed to make a stronger claim.

Classification vocabulary

Use the model's existing language

ApparitionMutationSelectionCooperationSpecialization
ContextExecutionVerificationCoordinationObservabilityEconomicsLearning

For model impact choose exactly one primary verdict: supports · refines · contradicts · inconclusive. Do not combine verdicts. If evidence both supports the model and changes its interpretation, choose refines and explain what changed. Evidence may span several stages or conditions; if uncertainty prevents a meaningful verdict, choose inconclusive. Out of scope is a scope result, not a fifth model verdict.

Agent instruction

A prompt you can use anywhere

Evaluate the following source against the Evolution of Agentic Engineering model at https://agenticengineering.science/ and its application protocol at https://agenticengineering.science/apply.html.

First check whether the source is in scope: it must contain observations about software engineering involving AI or coding agents, agentic engineering systems, or the engineering environments that support them. If it is not, return OUT OF SCOPE and stop; do not force an analogy into a model mapping.

If it is in scope, read the source before evaluating it. Separate what the source actually reports from your interpretation. Map only stages and Selection conditions supported by the evidence. Give exactly one primary model implication: SUPPORTS, REFINES, CONTRADICTS or INCONCLUSIVE. Do not combine verdicts. State what the source does not establish. Do not treat the model as proven, do not infer unreported scale or causality, and do not treat company claims as independent confirmation. Describe evidence as consistent with a hypothesis when appropriate; do not call a model condition predicted, confirmed or proven unless the source actually establishes that claim.

Epistemic rules

Keep the evidence stronger than the story

  • The model is a working model, not a prediction or established evolutionary sequence.
  • Check domain relevance before mapping. Similar vocabulary or structural analogy in another domain is not evidence for this model.
  • Observed facts must come from the evaluated source; model language belongs in interpretation.
  • Evidence may be consistent with a hypothesis in the model. Do not describe a condition as predicted, confirmed or proven unless the source actually establishes that claim.
  • Prefer first-party engineering evidence. Label secondary reporting as such.
  • A company's interpretation of its own results is evidence of what it reports, not independent validation.
  • Do not convert product capability into demonstrated organizational scale.
  • 1K-agent scale is a forcing function, not an industry benchmark.
  • Contradictory evidence deserves the same prominence as supporting evidence.
  • If an in-scope source does not contain enough evidence for a meaningful mapping, return INCONCLUSIVE.
