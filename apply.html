Test new evidence against the model

A protocol for placing public engineering evidence against the Evolution of Agentic Engineering without turning interpretation into observation.

Protocol

Observation first. Interpretation second.

01 · ACQUIRE

Read the original public source and the current model.

02 · OBSERVE

Extract only what the source reports.

03 · MAP

Map only active stages and supported Selection conditions.

04 · INTERPRET

Describe variation as a mechanism when relevant; do not turn it into a stage.

05 · TEST

Choose SUPPORTS, REFINES, CONTRADICTS or INCONCLUSIVE.

06 · BOUND

State what the source does not establish and the next useful observation.

Classification vocabulary

Use the v0.2 structure

ApparitionVariation / mutation · mechanismSelectionCooperationSpecialization

Only Apparition, Selection, Cooperation and Specialization are stages. Variation can be discussed in interpretation but must not be entered as a stage mapping.
