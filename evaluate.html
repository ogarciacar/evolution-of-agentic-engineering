Evaluation semantics

Counts are not votes.

UNOBSERVED means no evidence is mapped to the claim. EMERGING means SUPPORTS or REFINES evidence exists without contradiction or inconclusive mappings. CONTESTED means at least one mapped record is INCONCLUSIVE and none CONTRADICTS the claim. CHALLENGED means at least one record explicitly contradicts the claim.

Contradiction dominates the state: supporting volume cannot erase challenging evidence. Producer diversity and provenance remain properties of the underlying records rather than hidden numeric weights.
