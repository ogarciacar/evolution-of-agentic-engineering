How to interpret this working model

AgenticEngineering.science is a working, evidence-backed model for reasoning about how software engineering may evolve as coding agents become more capable, numerous, cooperative and specialized.

A working model by Orlando Garcia
Working model v0.2 · September 2026

It is not a maturity model, prediction engine, or catalogue of universal best practices. Evidence is kept distinct from interpretation, and practices are considered in the conditions that may select for them.

THINKING LENS

This model was developed using Daniel San Martín's Clarity model as a thinking lens, particularly Apparition, Selection, Cooperation and Specialization.

The application to agentic software engineering is my own.
